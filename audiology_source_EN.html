ABR INSTRUCTIONS

Here is some helpful information for you to know about your child's visit:

Due to the small room size, only one adult may be allowed into the testing room with your baby. Please do not bring any other children to the appointment, unless there is another adult who can be with them in the waiting room.

Do not apply lotion or gel on the child's head prior to this appointment.

Your baby will need to sleep for the hearing test. It is best if you do not allow your baby to sleep for one hour before the appointment. If your baby falls asleep right after eating, it may be helpful to wait until you arrive at Children's to feed your baby.
